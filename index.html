Guides

Quick strategy notes. Tap any guide to open the tips.

❄️
WHITEOUT SURVIVAL

Beginner Progression

Build a strong foundation and avoid wasting resources.

Read guide
  • Prioritize Furnace upgrades when requirements are ready; unlocks more progression.
  • Keep resource buildings and research moving, but don't spend every speedup at once.
  • Train troops regularly and use events to earn extra rewards.
  • Join an active alliance for help, gifts, rallies and protection.
  • Save premium currency and rare upgrade materials for planned goals.
🐻
WHITEOUT SURVIVAL · BEAR TRAP

Bear Trap Rally Basics

Coordinate rallies for better damage and rewards.

Read guide
  • Use your strongest damage-focused rally captain and the heroes recommended for your generation.
  • Join rallies with the best available joiner heroes that provide troop buffs; avoid sending random heroes.
  • Follow alliance rally timing and keep troops available for the trap.
  • Use a consistent troop ratio recommended by your alliance or current event meta.
  • Compare damage reports and adjust heroes or troop composition between attempts.
⚔️
WHITEOUT SURVIVAL

Rally Leader & Joiner

Understand your role before joining a rally.

Read guide
  • Rally leaders should use their strongest buff-focused hero setup for the rally objective.
  • Joiners should place the requested buff hero in the first hero slot if alliance rules require it.
  • Send the troop type and amount requested by the rally leader.
  • Don't start competing rallies if your alliance is coordinating one target.
  • Check alliance instructions because hero and troop meta can change by generation.
🏰
KINGSHOT

Progression & Resources

Grow efficiently and prepare for events.

Read guide
  • Complete daily missions and limited-time activities to collect progression rewards.
  • Upgrade core buildings as required for your next milestone instead of upgrading everything evenly.
  • Keep troops training and use stamina on activities that give useful rewards.
  • Join an active alliance to benefit from assistance and coordinated events.
  • Plan resource spending around upgrades and event goals.

WILD WATER WORLD

Starter Tips

Learn the basics and make early progress.

Read guide
  • Follow the game's early objectives to unlock core features.
  • Collect available daily and event rewards regularly.
  • Upgrade your main progression tools before spreading resources too thin.
  • Explore new areas when your character and equipment are ready.
  • Use the in-game event information to decide where to focus.
